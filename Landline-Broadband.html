How a sign-up call runs

Five steps. No shortcuts.

The same sequence, every time because that consistency is what makes the call defensible if anyone ever asks to hear it back.

01

Open & identify

Agent identifies Knight Tech Solution, the client provider, and the reason for the call before anything else is discussed.

Mandatory disclosure
02

Current setup check

We confirm the customer's current landline/broadband provider, contract status, and connection needs before presenting any switching or sign-up opportunity.

No blind pitching
03

Package comparison

The agent explains speeds, call allowances, contract terms, and estimated savings using provider-approved scripts, ensuring every comparison is clear, accurate, and easy to understand.

Provider-approved wording
04

Consent & verification

Explicit verbal consent is captured and timestamped before any sign-up or switch request moves forward.

Recorded checkpoint
05

Warm transfer or booking

Confirmed leads are transferred live or booked for provider follow-up, with the full call attached to the record.

Handover, not hand-off
> Agent: "Hi, this is Sam calling on behalf of [Provider Name]. I'm calling today regarding your current landline and broadband service. Is now a convenient time to speak?"
> System: consent checkpoint logged
> Agent: "Can I confirm what speed and call package you're currently on?"

Common questions

Before you sign off on a campaign.

How long before we're live?

Typically two weeks from script sign-off to first live dial, including agent training and system setup.

Can we listen to calls in real time?

Yes. Supervisors and your team can join the floor from day one, not just review recordings afterward.

Who owns script changes once we're live?

Your compliance team. Any revision is proposed by us but only goes live once you've signed off on the exact wording.

Do you handle both residential and business sign-ups?

Yes, we run separate scripts and eligibility checks for each, since the disclosures required aren't the same.
