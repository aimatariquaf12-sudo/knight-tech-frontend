How a sign-up call runs

Five steps. No shortcuts.

The same sequence, every time because that consistency is what makes the call defensible if anyone ever asks to hear it back.

01

Open & identify

Agent identifies KnightVolt, the client water provider, and the reason for the call before anything else is discussed.

Mandatory disclosure
02

Current supply check

We confirm the household or business's current water supplier, meter status, and property type before offering anything.

No blind pitching
03

Plan comparison

Agent walks through the approved plan script standing charge, metered vs unmetered, billing cycle stated plainly.

Provider-approved wording
04

Consent & verification

Explicit verbal consent is captured and timestamped before any enrolment request moves forward.

Recorded checkpoint
05

Confirmation or booking

Confirmed sign-ups are processed live or booked for provider follow-up, with the full call attached to the record.

Handover, not hand-off
> Agent: "Hi, this is Amel calling on behalf of your water supplier about your current household plan — is now an okay time?"
> System: consent checkpoint logged
> Agent: "Can I confirm your property is currently on a metered supply?"

Common questions

Before you sign off on a campaign.

How long before we're live?

Typically two weeks from script sign-off to first live dial, including agent training and system setup.

Can we listen to calls in real time?

Yes. Supervisors and your team can join the floor from day one, not just review recordings afterward.

Who owns script changes once we're live?

Your compliance team. Any revision is proposed by us but only goes live once you've signed off on the exact wording.

Do you handle both residential and SME sign-ups?

Yes, we run separate scripts and eligibility checks for each, since the disclosures required aren't the same.
