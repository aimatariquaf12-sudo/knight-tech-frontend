How a call runs

Five steps. No shortcuts.

The same sequence, every time because that consistency is what makes the call defensible if anyone ever asks to hear it back.

01

Open & identify

Agent identifies Knight Tech Solution, the payment provider, and the reason for the call before anything else is discussed.

Mandatory disclosure
02

Business eligibility check

We confirm the business type, current card turnover, and existing payment set-up before presenting any card machine options.

No blind pitching
03

Card machine walkthrough

The agent explains device types, transaction rates, settlement times, and contract terms using supplier-approved scripts, ensuring every option is clear, accurate, and easy to understand.

Supplier-approved wording
04

Consent & verification

Explicit verbal consent is captured and timestamped before any application moves forward.

Recorded checkpoint
05

Warm transfer or onboarding

Confirmed leads are transferred live or booked for provider onboarding, with the full call attached to the record.

Handover, not hand-off
> Agent: "Hi, this is Sam calling on behalf of [Provider Name]. I'm calling today about how you currently take card payments. Is now a convenient time to speak?"
> System: consent checkpoint logged
> Agent: "Can I confirm roughly how many card transactions your business takes each month?"

Common questions

Before you sign off on a campaign.

How long before we're live?

Typically two weeks from script sign-off to first live dial, including agent training and system setup.

Can we listen to calls in real time?

Yes. Supervisors and your team can join the floor from day one, not just review recordings afterward.

Who owns script changes once we're live?

Your compliance team. Any revision is proposed by us but only goes live once you've signed off on the exact wording.

Do you qualify businesses before pitching a card machine?

Yes, we confirm business type and card turnover first, since that determines which providers and devices are actually a fit.
